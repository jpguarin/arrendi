# Arrendi — Colors & Fonts

## Brand colors

Fixed values used in the logo, illustration and marketing pieces.

| Name | Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Azul confianza | `brand-navy` | `#14264C` | Logo, titles, brand blocks |
| Verde tranquilidad | `brand-green` | `#28B779` | Isotipo windows, dot of the i, accents (never as text on light backgrounds) |
| Amarillo claridad | `brand-yellow` | `#FFC857` | Illustration, highlights (never as text) |
| Marfil cálido | `brand-ivory` | `#F4F1E8` | Brand background, text/logo on navy |

## Fonts

Both are free under the SIL Open Font License and available on Google Fonts.

| Family | Role | Weights | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Red Hat Display** | Logo, titles, hero amounts | 700, 800 (logo: 900) | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Red+Hat+Display) |
| **Inter** | Interface: body, forms, tables, statuses, PDFs | 400, 500, 600 | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) |

```css
--font-display: "Red Hat Display", Inter, system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-sans: Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
```
